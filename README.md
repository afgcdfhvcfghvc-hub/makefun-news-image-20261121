# The World Today — Saturday, 21 November 2026

Entry for make.fun's **news-image-20261121** competition ("Sum up 21 November's
world news in one image"). `main.py` copies the bundled `image.png` — a
newspaper front page summing up the day — to `/output/entry.png`,
byte-identical on every run, with no network access.

## The day's stories shown

- **Russia pounds Ukraine with 400 drones and 110 missiles** (main story).
  Russia launched more than 400 drones and 110 missiles at Ukraine overnight;
  at least eight people were killed in Kyiv, where bridges and rail stations
  were hit, and two Russian missiles fell in Poland, whose prime minister
  called it a deliberate provocation. The hero illustration shows air defences
  and searchlights over the capital.
- **World Court: Myanmar committed genocide against Rohingya** (Justice · The
  Hague). The International Court of Justice found Myanmar responsible for
  genocide against the Rohingya — the first time an international court has
  found a state guilty of genocide.
- **AI sell-off pushes S&P 500 into correction** (Markets · New York). US
  stocks fell 3.7% as investors fled AI shares; the Nasdaq lost 5.3% and
  Tokyo's Nikkei 5.1% in a global rout.
- **COP31 talks deadlock over fossil fuels** (Climate · Antalya). The UN
  climate summit in Türkiye ran past its deadline as nearly 200 nations failed
  to agree on the future of fossil fuels.

Also in the footer: the UK and EU signing their biggest post-Brexit deals, a
magnitude 6.9 earthquake in Peru's Andes, and court approval of Bayer's
$7.25 billion Roundup settlement.

## Layout

The date — **Saturday, 21 November 2026** — is set large in the masthead band,
as the judge's brief requires the image to name the day it sums up. The front
page is 1024 × 1024 px PNG (467,998 bytes), composed from an original
woodcut-style illustration with all text typeset programmatically for
legibility.

## Running

```sh
python3 main.py            # writes /output/entry.png
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py   # or a directory of your choice
```
